Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

SitterLog is an app for independent pet sitters to keep records of their visits, clients, mileage, expenses and income. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: we do not collect it. There is no SitterLog account and no SitterLog server, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What you store in the app

You may enter information about yourself and your clients, including names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, door and gate codes, pet care, medical and veterinary details, visit times and notes, mileage, expenses and receipt photos, payments, invoices and business details. You decide what to enter.

Where it is kept

  • On your device. Your records are stored in the app on your iPhone (or iPad), protected by iOS data protection. An optional app lock asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
  • In your private iCloud, if you use iCloud. When you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs your records through Apple's CloudKit to your own private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices and survive a lost phone. That data belongs to your Apple Account and counts against your iCloud storage. We cannot see or access it. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Local recovery copies. The app keeps a daily backup file for up to seven days and a copy of its database for up to three days, on your device only, so a damaged store can be recovered. A record you delete can remain in these copies until they roll over.

What leaves your device

  • iCloud sync, as above, only to your own iCloud.
  • Addresses you look up. When you search for an address or calculate a route's mileage, the address text is sent to Apple Maps to return results. The app does not use your location and never asks for location permission.
  • Things you choose to share. Summary cards, invoices, CSV and tax exports, backups and database copies leave the device only when you share or save them. Backups and database copies contain your records — including door codes, addresses, phone numbers and private notes — as ordinary, unencrypted text, so keep them somewhere private.

We do not sell, rent or share your information, because we never receive it.

Photos and Face ID

Photos you attach are chosen with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you select. They are resized and stored without their original location metadata. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; the app never receives your biometric data.

Deleting your information

  • Delete any record in the app; with iCloud on, the deletion syncs to your other devices.
  • To remove everything from a device, delete the app.
  • To remove the iCloud copy, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then iCloud ▸ Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage) ▸ SitterLog, and delete its data.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, but we are happy to help you through these steps.

Children

SitterLog is a business tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Tax figures

The app's summaries are prepared from the records you enter. They are not tax, legal or accounting advice. Review them, and consult a tax professional about your own situation.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever began collecting information, this policy and the App Store privacy label would say so before that version is released.
