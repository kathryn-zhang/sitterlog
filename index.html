Your pet-sitting records, kept like a business.

SitterLog is the record-keeping app for independent pet sitters. Log each visit, send pet parents a summary card, and keep every mile, expense and payment ready for tax time.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone

Free · No account · Nothing collected

Ready for tax time

Business miles from the addresses you type, expenses with receipt photos, and money received by the year it arrived. Bring one tidy summary to whoever prepares your return.

Look like a business

After each visit, share a card with the photo, the checklist and your note. Send a clean invoice to your independent clients when it's time to be paid.

Private by design

Client addresses and door codes stay on your phone and in your own private iCloud. No SitterLog account, no server, no ads and no tracking — the App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected".

What SitterLog is not

It isn't a booking platform or a marketplace, and it doesn't process payments. It never logs in to or fetches anything from a pet-care platform, and it isn't affiliated with one. It keeps records and summarizes them; it doesn't give tax, legal or accounting advice.
