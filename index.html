SitterLog

The record-keeping app for independent pet sitters.

Log each visit with its care checklist and notes, send pet parents a tidy summary card, and keep your mileage, expenses and income in one place — ready when tax time comes.

  • Visits, recurring bookings and per-client care checklists
  • Summary cards for pet parents
  • Mileage from the addresses you type — no location tracking
  • Expenses with receipt photos, money received and invoices
  • A tax-year summary to bring to your accountant

No account and nothing collected by us: your records stay on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.

SitterLog is not a booking platform and is not affiliated with any pet-care marketplace. It does not give tax, legal or accounting advice.
