About SitterLog

Built by a cat sitter, for the records every independent sitter keeps.

I'm [your first name], and I've been sitting cats [in your area / for how long]. Some of my clients find me through a booking platform; others are neighbors and friends of clients who book me directly.

The cat care was never the hard part. The records were. Each visit meant feeding notes, a photo for the pet parent, the miles I drove and sometimes a bag of litter I paid for. For my direct clients nothing kept track of who had paid. When tax time came, I was rebuilding my year from texts, my camera roll and bank statements.

I looked for an app. What I found was either software for pet-care companies with staff, priced to match, or another platform that wanted to sit between me and my clients. I wanted something smaller: a place to write down what I did, what I drove, what I spent and what I was paid, and to hand pet parents something nice at the end of a visit.

So I built SitterLog for my own records, and I use it after every visit. [One sentence on what changed for you, e.g. the first tax season with it.]

What I promise

  • Your records are yours. There's no SitterLog account and no SitterLog server. Everything stays on your phone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.
  • No middleman. SitterLog will never book clients, take a cut, or connect to a pet-care platform for you.
  • A real person answers. I read every message sent to sitterlog.support@gmail.com.

If you sit pets independently and something in the app could fit your work better, I'd like to hear about it.
