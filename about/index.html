About SitterLog

Built by a cat sitter, for the records every independent sitter keeps.

Hi, I’m Kathryn, a cat sitter based in Sunnyvale, California. I’ve been cat sitting for over a year. I started after losing my beloved cat, DumDum, when I realized how much I missed caring for these mysterious little creatures. These days, I’m cat mom to Dooblu, my chaotic orange boy, and I still love looking after other people’s cats while they’re away.

I built SitterLog around the everyday needs of my own sitting work: keeping detailed notes, staying on top of to-do lists, and sharing thoughtful updates so cat parents can enjoy their time away with peace of mind. It also helps me track earnings, manage invoices, and keep my records organized for tax time.

SitterLog has helped me spend less time piecing together records and more time focusing on the cats in my care.

What I promise

  • Your records are yours. There's no SitterLog account and no SitterLog server. Everything stays on your phone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.
  • No middleman. SitterLog will never book clients, take a cut, or connect to a pet-care platform for you.
  • A real person answers. I read every message sent to sitterlog.support@gmail.com.

If you sit pets independently and something in the app could fit your work better, I'd like to hear about it.
