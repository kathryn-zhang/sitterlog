Support

SitterLog is made by one independent developer, who reads every message.

Contact

Email sitterlog.support@gmail.com. The quickest way is Settings ▸ Contact Support in the app, which fills in your app version. If iCloud sync shows an error code in Settings, tap Copy beside it and paste it into your message. Please don't send backup files or screenshots showing clients' door codes.

You can expect a reply within two business days.

Common questions

My records haven't appeared on my new phone. With iCloud on, records download by themselves, and a large history can take a while. Keep the app open on Wi-Fi and check Settings ▸ Sync. Wait for the download to finish before restoring from a backup file.

How do I back up? Settings ▸ Data ▸ Back Up Data saves a file you can keep in Files or iCloud Drive. The app also keeps a daily backup on the device. Restore from Backup adds records from a file without overwriting what is already there.

Is SitterLog connected to Rover or other platforms? No. It never logs in to or fetches anything from a booking platform.

Are the tax figures ready to file? They are a summary of the records you entered, to review and bring to whoever prepares your return. They are not tax advice.

How do I delete my data? See Deleting your information in the privacy policy.
